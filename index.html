Welcome to UCTTC

Empowering Youth Through Quality IT Education

Unique Computer Technical Training Center (UCTTC) is a premier government-certified training institution dedicated to providing high-quality computer and IT education in Bangladesh. Since our establishment, we have been committed to transforming students into skilled professionals ready for the global job market.

Our state-of-the-art facilities, experienced instructors, and industry-relevant curriculum ensure that every student receives practical, hands-on training that prepares them for real-world challenges.
